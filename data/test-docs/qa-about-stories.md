# Q&A About the Three Stories

## Story 1: The Last Signal from Luna-7

**Q1: Who is the main character and what is her mission?**
A1: Commander Elena Vasquez leads a rescue mission to Luna-7, humanity's first Moon colony that has gone silent for three days. Her brother Marco, an astrophysicist, is among the 247 colonists trapped there.

**Q2: How did Marco signal for help, and why was it effective?**
A2: Marco played Chopin's Nocturne in E-flat major — their shared childhood song — on the colony's emergency broadcast system loop. A standard distress beacon would be ignored after the first day, but music made people stop and listen.

**Q3: What caused Luna-7's communication failure?**
A3: A meteorite strike kicked up a cloud of metallic lunar regolith (iron and nickel dust) that coated the communication antenna array. The beacon's lower frequency could penetrate, but voice and data could not.

**Q4: How did Elena violate Space Council protocols?**
A4: By her own count, she violated twenty-three protocols including: unscheduled rescue without authorization, using a non-landing transport vessel for lunar approach, and personally conducting a surface EVA without proper mission clearance.

**Q5: What is the symbolic significance of the piano music?**
A5: The Chopin piece represents the sibling bond between Elena and Marco. It transforms from a distress signal into a symbol of hope, and finally back into ordinary music — marking the colony's return to normal life.

## Story 2: The Clockmaker of Venice

**Q1: What is Antonio Moretti's profession and philosophy?**
A1: Antonio is a 73-year-old clockmaker in Venice who repairs mechanical timepieces. He believes digital clocks are "soulless" and that real clocks have "gears and springs, with pendulums that swing like the heartbeat of time itself."

**Q2: What was special about the pocket watch brought by the young woman?**
A2: The silver-cased pocket watch belonged to Rosa's grandmother, who died in a fire. In her final two minutes, the grandmother reached for the watch — her wedding gift from her husband — rather than trying to escape. The watch hands froze at 3:47, the moment she died.

**Q3: How did Antonio repair the watch, and what did he add?**
A3: He spent three weeks hand-filing replacement gears, straightening the warped mainplate using his grandfather's technique, and sourcing parts from his 19th-century collection. On the inside lid, he engraved: "Some moments are meant to last forever."

**Q4: Why didn't Antonio charge Rosa for the repair?**
A4: He told her, "You already paid. You reminded me why I do this." The repair reconnected him to the purpose of his craft — preserving meaningful moments in time.

**Q5: What revelation does Antonio have at the end of the story?**
A5: Hearing Venice's ancient clock tower strike the hour, he realizes time is "something that connects us — to our past, to each other, to the moments that define who we are." He leaves the watch set permanently at 3:47.

## Story 3: The Garden Beneath the Waves

**Q1: What is the Nereus Dome and where is it located?**
A1: It is humanity's first underwater farm — a geodesic dome of reinforced glass and titanium sitting sixty meters below the surface of the Philippine Sea. It uses artificial sunlight, reverse osmosis, and synthetic soil made from marine sediment.

**Q2: What was Dr. Yuki Tanaka's key innovation?**
A2: She developed a synthetic growth medium from processed marine sediment enriched with minerals from deep-sea hydrothermal vents. This "soil" is three times more fertile than the best terrestrial farmland.

**Q3: What was the first commercial crop, and what was the yield?**
A3: Strawberries. The first harvest yielded 412 kilograms from a single section, grown without any fresh water from the surface. The strawberries were described as incredibly sweet with exceptional flavor depth.

**Q4: What are the advantages of underwater farming?**
A4: Near-infinite water supply via desalination, stable temperatures (insulated by the seabed), enormous available space (oceans cover 70% of Earth), no weather dependency, and solar power from surface panels.

**Q5: What marine life does Yuki encounter, and what does it symbolize?**
A5: A twelve-meter whale shark glides over the dome, appearing curious about the "strange bubble of light and life." This encounter symbolizes the harmony between human innovation and the natural ocean environment.
